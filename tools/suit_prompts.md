# Suit pictures for СЭЛФИ ШИНОСНОМА — prompts

These make "invisible mannequin" clothing photos: the clothes only, no person, collar closed, centred, on white.
The app keeps the customer's real head and neck and cuts along the collar, so every picture must follow the same layout.

## How to use (Gemini or any image tool)
1. Paste the **base prompt**, then add one line from the list below (one picture per suit).
2. Choose a **portrait 3:4** picture and the **highest resolution** available.
3. Check each result before keeping it:
   - no person, no skin, no hair, no mannequin visible
   - the neck opening is in the **exact centre** at the top, collar closed (men: tie knot at the collar)
   - shoulders reach (or nearly reach) both side edges; the picture is cut off below the chest
   - plain pure white background, no shadows on it, no text or watermark
4. Download the original file (not a screenshot) and save it as `m01.png` … `m10.png`, `w01.png` … `w10.png`
   in the folder `PassportPic/suits-new/`.

## Base prompt
> Invisible ghost mannequin product photograph of [OUTFIT], front view, perfectly straight and symmetrical,
> no person, no mannequin, no skin, empty neck opening exactly centred at the top of the frame, collar neatly closed,
> shoulders reaching the left and right edges, cropped just below the chest, pure white seamless background,
> soft even studio lighting, no shadows, sharp fabric detail, high resolution, portrait 3:4, no text, no watermark.
